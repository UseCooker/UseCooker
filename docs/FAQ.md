# FAQ

### Do I have to launch on COOKER?

No. Existing Pump coins can redirect creator fees and join the engine.

### Why do claims show on the chart?

Visibility is the point. Pressure that nobody can see doesn’t change behavior.

### What changes after migration?

The split adds a locked LP leg. Buybacks still burn. COOKER’s share stays at 20%.

### Are buybacks burned?

Yes. Bought coin and COOKER tokens are burned. Deposited LP tokens are burned so that liquidity cannot be pulled.

### Can cashback coins attach?

No. Cashback coins keep fees with traders. Holder-reward coins pay holders. COOKER needs a fee recipient it can claim.

### Can the fee recipient be changed later?

Follow Pump’s share-config rules. COOKER is designed for locked routing once attached.

### Is this repo the full product source?

No. This is the public protocol surface — docs and the `protocol/` code. The live product is [usecooker.xyz](https://usecooker.xyz).

### Where do I see cooks happening?

[usecooker.xyz/cooks](https://usecooker.xyz/cooks) and the corresponding on-chain transactions.
